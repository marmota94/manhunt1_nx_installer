## The port release it serves

`release/manhunt1-nx-v1.0.0.zip` is the unmodified port-only release of
[manhunt1_nx](https://github.com/marmota94/manhunt1_nx/releases/tag/v1.0.0)
(SHA-256 `5bd3a778fbe685aadc2395f083f9a1b0d1d414b7c70b590a2648df8a8b33bc35`).
Its licences ship inside it, under `switch/manhunt1_nx/LICENSES/`, and the
installer copies every one of them into each SD package:

- `LGPL-2.1.txt`: `manhunt.nro` statically links an Xbox audio emulator (from
  xemu, through xboxrecomp) licensed under the GNU LGPL, version 2.1 or later.
- `SOURCE-OFFER.txt`: where the complete corresponding source of that library
  is published (`manhunt1-nx-v1.0.0-lgpl-source.zip` on the same release).
- `MIT-xboxrecomp.txt`: the MIT licence of xboxrecomp.
